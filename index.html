Chapter 11: Bedtime Stories - Love Of Things May Broken

February 14, 2020 — Valentine's Day Special

Once upon a time, things weren’t supposed to be broken, like the person named Alitobee. He felt that things bought with hard-earned money should be loved and never wasted or stolen by kidnappers and swindlers. That’s why: "Love Of Things May Not Be Broken".

The young girl, Neyri, born on Valentine’s Day, loved the things she owned—her belt bags, cellphones, bank accounts, and ID. She kept them safely inside her belt bag while going to college.

One fateful evening near the Dark Traffic Lights and the Gasoline Station, Neyri encountered suspicious strangers—the infamous Modus Operandi. They tricked her with fake stories about an injured friend in the hospital and swindled her into surrendering her belt bag under threats of fake police charges.

After realizing she was hypnotized and swindled out of her items and a promised $50,000 reward, Neyri filed a blotter at the local Barangay Hall. With guidance and reprimand from Alitobee, Neyri learned the essential rule of survival: Never trust or talk to strangers in dark alleys!
